# Protocol Parameters

All constants live in `src/libraries/BazaarTypes.sol`, `src/libraries/RiskParamsLib.sol`, and the contracts that use them. Values below are verified against the code.

## Order book & matching

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `MIN_ORDER_AMOUNT` | $5 notional (full closes exempt) |
| `MAX_SLIPPAGE_BP` | 1000 (10%) — cap on the slippage bound of `Market`, `StopLoss` and `TakeProfit` orders |
| `MAX_PLACEMENT_DEVIATION_BP` | 5000 — a `Limit` order's price must be within ±50% of the oracle price when it is created or amended; `StopLimit` is exempt (its limit anchors to its trigger) |
| `MIN_ORDER_LIFETIME_BLOCKS` / max | 240 blocks (~1 min) / ~1 year — the minimum holds for every entry into the book: an amendment that moves an order (a new price or a larger size) needs that much life left, and a StopLimit that fires with less keeps its remainder live outside the book instead of resting it |
| `MAX_ACTIVE_LIMIT_ORDERS_PER_USER` | 32 (Limit + StopLimit; `IOC` and `FOK` limits never rest and take no slot) |
| TP/SL per position | 1 each |
| `MAX_CANCELS_PER_CALL` | 200 |
| `MAX_AMENDS_PER_CALL` | 32 — one batch can requote every resting order a user may hold; the batch is all or nothing |
| `MAX_WALK_STEPS` | 32 — ceiling on a walk's node budget (`MatchHints.maxSteps`; 0 selects the ceiling, which relayed orders and keeper-fired stops always run) and on the insertion-hint repair walk |
| Insertion hints | up to 4 predecessor candidates (`MatchHints.pred..pred4`), outside the EIP-712 payload; `amendOrders` instead carries up to two per amendment inside its signed id word |
| `MAX_TRIGGER_EXECS_PER_CALL` | 20 stops per `executeTriggeredOrders` call |
| Trigger bounty | owner-chosen per order (high 128 bits of `OrderSpec.triggerPrice`, 18-decimal and a whole number of USDC's 6-decimal units; zero on every other order type); escrowed from the wallet at creation, paid to executors pro rata to what they fill, the whole remainder once the order completes or rests in the book (an execution in the creation block of an order the stale fallback left unarmed pays the owner), refunded to the owner's claimable balance when the order ends unexecuted |
| `TERMINAL_SETTLEMENT_TIP` | $0.05 per position settled for someone else at the fixed terminal price — from the position's remaining collateral, insurance topping up only for positions worth at least $5 at the settlement price; after finalize from the insurers' residual, bankrupt stragglers of at least $5 only |
| `LIQ_MAX_SLIPPAGE_BP` | 500 — cap on the oracle leg of the unload bound: the vault takes makers within ±min(5%, MMR) of oracle at once, where MMR is the lower of the current rate and the lowest rate recorded over the past day, the hour in progress included (so a one-minute spike in live open interest cannot widen the band), and makers beyond that edge which beat the estates' fund-neutral price once the floor has opened to them, within the fund's headroom above its reserve; the same band edge is the ADL release level |
| `UNLOAD_FLOOR_DECAY` | 3 min — the unload floor slides linearly from the band edge to the fund-neutral price (held from crossing the estates' bankruptcy price) over this long, measured from the inventory's size-weighted seizure time, so the estates' residual equity is offered to competing bids before the vault sells it at bankruptcy |
| `ADL_RESERVE_BP` | 1,000 — 10% of the insurance target on open interest, kept out of unloads and the ADL gate for the losses no bound can refuse. The open interest is the lower of the live figure and its lowest hourly sample over the past 24 h (`Vault.trailingOiSize`), so it counts toward the reserve only once held for a day; live open interest alone until the pair has a sampled hour that old |
| `LIQ_INLINE_UNLOAD_STEPS` | 16 — node budget for the unload walk inside `liquidate()` |
| `LIQ_GAP_PRIOR_FLOOR_BP` | 100 — 1% of per-side open interest, the least weight the prior carries when a liquidation batch is folded into the slippage and drift EMAs; a batch enters at its size against the larger of this and the weight earlier batches accumulated, so it moves the readings by its share of the book: a dust unload by a fraction of a basis point, and many small unloads as one of their total size would |
| Integrator fee | 0.25 bp maker + 0.25 bp taker — only on orders naming an integrator |

## Margin

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `BASE_IMR_BP` | 300 (3%) |
| Stress move `M` | the risk reading margin and the fund target are both anchored on. `V` = 3% × vol multiplier; continuous `M` = V/2 + unload-slippage EMA; non-continuous `M` = max(V, reopen-gap EMA) + unload-slippage EMA. Clamped to 2% … 40% (`MIN_STRESS_MOVE_BP` … `MAX_STRESS_MOVE_BP`, exactly half the IMR clamps) |
| Volatility multiplier | 1×→6× (through 3× at 100% ann. vol, capped at ~157%) |
| Unload-slippage term | ADDS its EMA to `M` at full size rather than multiplying: slippage is the execution cost of closing inherited inventory, measured fill-against-oracle, and maintenance margin has to pay for getting out. The value read fades harmonically with the age of the liquidations behind it — a single episode's reading halves at 3 days (`LIQUIDATION_GAP_EMA_TAU`) — and a batch blends against the faded value, moving the fade's anchor toward the present only by its share of the reading, so a pair whose book recovers returns to its volatility curve on its own and dust unloads can neither restart the fade nor round the reading away (it is stored to a fraction of a basis point). Each fill is recorded within [0, `LIQ_MAX_SLIPPAGE_BP`] before it is weighted: a fill better than the oracle counts as zero cost at full weight (the gain is already in the fund, and banking it as credit would count it twice), and the ceiling is the unload bound's own — a fill lands further out only when the market sits that far past bankruptcy in the fund's favor, which measures the walk's latitude rather than the cost of a forced exit. A batch is weighed against max(accumulated weight, 1% of per-side open interest) (`LIQ_GAP_PRIOR_FLOOR_BP`), so it moves the reading by its share of the book: a dust unload by a fraction of a basis point, an estate worth x% of the book x/(1+x) of the way |
| Realized-drift term | the companion leg, measured oracle-against-bankruptcy-price: how far the market ran past the level margin was set to survive while the vault held the inventory. It reaches the fund target, not `M`, and competes with the forecast leg by `max` rather than summing — realized drift is a draw from the very distribution `M` estimates, so adding them would size the fund for one tail twice. Shares the slippage leg's weight, its floor, the tradeable clock and the 3-day fade, on a fade anchor of its own. Each fill is floored at zero before it is weighted — an oracle still short of bankruptcy is a gain the unload already paid into the fund — but never capped: a large reading is a genuine overrun |
| `FADE_SUSPEND_MIN_GAP` | 12 h — on a session pair, a stored-price gap this long counts as a market closure, and both liquidation legs' fade anchors and the weight's batch clock are pushed forward across it instead of ageing; a shorter gap counts as trading time. Between stores the readings hold still, so a gap is judged only once the next price lands. A liquidation stores its fresh price or needs one stored within `MAX_LIQUIDATION_PRICE_AGE`, so such a gap is time in which none happened — and, on a pair whose vault holds at least $1,250 of inventory and whose fund holds more than $5,000 of free capital, which the refresh bounty keeps stored every 5 minutes, none could have — while the fade reads absence of liquidations as evidence the level is holding. Sits above the longest session (6.5 h) and below the shortest closure (17.5 h overnight, 65.5 h weekend). A continuously traded pair counts every gap as trading time, since its feed publishes around the clock and a silence in its store is a quiet book, not a closure |
| Insurance multiplier | 1×→3× as the fund goes target → empty. Applied to IMR (and so to MMR, its half) after `M`, and never to the target |
| Non-continuous multiplier | 2× — scales the warmup floor; past warmup the same closure prior is the whole-vs-half vol curve in `M` |
| Reopen-gap leg | a peak-hold of \|reopen return\| across ≥ 1 h price gaps (closures, halts, trading breaks; `REOPEN_GAP_MIN_STALENESS`), harmonic decay τ = 130 d (`REOPEN_GAP_EMA_TAU`), capped at 25% (`REOPEN_GAP_EMA_CAP_BP`) — so MMR covers a demonstrated reopen up to the cap, and the same number reaches the fund target at full size. τ is set against the recurrence interval: a quarterly gapper still holds half its peak when the next one lands. The cap sits below the stress-move ceiling so a cap-sized gap leaves the fund-health multiplier room to act, and a repeat past it settles through ADL at the estates' bankruptcy price rather than being pre-funded in every position's margin |
| IMR | 2 × `M` × insurance multiplier, clamped to 4% … 80% (25× … 1.25× leverage) |
| Warmup floor | 20% IMR (40% for non-continuously traded pairs) until the pair is 15 days old (`WARMUP_DURATION`, three time constants of the variance EMA) *and* has stored 25,000 prices (`WARMUP_PRICE_UPDATE_THRESHOLD`). A floor, never an override — a pair that earns more margin during warmup keeps it |
| MMR | IMR / 2 |
| `IMR_UPDATE_COOLDOWN` | 1 min — the stored IMR/MMR is recomputed at most once a minute; `liquidate()` judges at the rate its own price gives, capped by the stored rate inside the cooldown |
| MMR grace | 24 h: 25 hourly samples, each the lowest rate in force during its hour; every position, however recently opened, is judged against the lowest rate recorded from 24 h before the mark to the present, the hour in progress included, or the current rate if lower, so a rise must hold for the whole day to bite and a fall counts at once; a pair whose samples do not reach back a day yet uses the lowest rate it has recorded. The same ring samples per-side open interest, the lowest seen each hour, for the trailing figure the reserve is sized on |
| `MAX_STALE_DEVIATION_BP` | 1000 (±10% stale-fill band) |
| Stale withdrawal gate | a position holder's withdrawal requires a fresh price (reverts on the stale fallback); flat and order-only accounts are exempt, deposits unaffected |
| `MIN_COLLATERAL_AMOUNT` | $5 minimum collateral deposit |
| Retained-collateral floor | a position-holding account must keep margin in collateral proper (cash, not equity): `mmrBp` of current notional on continuously traded pairs, `imrBp` on session-bound pairs. Guarantees the fee that closes the position is always payable, and keeps margin in money that survives a reversal — paper profit evaporates in the same move it would need to buffer, and across a reopen gap no liquidation can interpose |

## Funding

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `FUNDING_INTERVAL` | 1 h |
| Rate ceiling | ±0.5% per hour on session-bound pairs (`MAX_FUNDING_RATE_SESSION`), ±0.1% on continuously traded ones (`MAX_FUNDING_RATE_CONTINUOUS`) — a session pair accrues only while its oracle is fresh, so it needs more per accruing hour for the same weekly force |
| Premium dampening | ÷ 8 (session) / ÷ 40 (continuous) — derived from each ceiling against the shared mark band, never mixed across pair types, so the clamp is never reached from strictly inside the band |
| `MAKER_EXPOSURE_BLOCKS` | 40 L2 blocks (~10 s) — a fill enters the mark only when its resting side was a bid above the index or an ask below it, weighted by how long that order had rested at its current price and size: full weight at 40 blocks, pro rata before, zero in the block it was placed — and at no more premium than the order stood at when it entered the book (`restedPremiumPpm`, millionths of the index, rounded up, capped at the band; zero, so the fill does not count at all, for an order that entered on the near side, at the index, or on the stale fallback). Both stamps are rewritten by an amendment that reprices or grows the order and kept by one that shrinks it |
| Mark tallies | The mark's premium is a volume tally over a premium tally: each qualifying fill (one update per matching call, fed the exposure-weighted VWAP of the walk's qualifying fills) adds its weighted notional to the first and that notional times its premium over the index to the second, and the premium is the second over the larger of the first and the floor, `VOLUME_FLOOR_BP` = 2% of per-side open-interest notional. One call may add at most `MAX_ALPHA` = 25% of the volume it is weighed against, so no call moves the reading more than a quarter of the way to its print; a quote under the floor reads its own share of its premium, and a stream holds its volume above the floor only while its fills arrive more often than about every six and a half minutes. Fills that do not qualify neither move the mark nor feed its volume |
| `MAX_MARK_DEVIATION_BP` | 400 — fills clamped to ±4% of index before their premium enters the mark; the reading, a weighted average of clamped prints, never leaves the band |
| `MARK_DECAY_PERIOD` | 1 h — both tallies run in a straight line to zero, ending this long after their anchor. While the volume stands above the floor the ratio holds; under it the reading fades with the premium tally. The mark is the index carried by the premium, so it moves with the index. A print moves the anchor toward the present only by its share of the volume, so what was there keeps its age, a dust print buys under a second and moves nothing, and a print against the premium extends the line exactly as much as it dilutes it. A fill enters the mark only while the stored index it is clamped and measured against is no older than `MAX_PRICE_STALENESS` (3 s), so a fill on the stale fallback neither moves the mark nor dates it as freshly traded. Each price store charges its window at the time-average of the premium the line read across it |
| Oracle-gap guards | a price published more than 30 min after the last accrual accrues only the 30 min before it (`MAX_FUNDING_AGE`); one more than 12 h after (`MAX_FUNDING_GAP`) skips the dark period entirely |

## Liquidation, ADL & insurance

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Liquidation keeper reward | $0.05 (`MIN_LIQUIDATOR_REWARD`) for each position seized or deferred liquidation newly recorded, or max($0.05, 2 bp (`LIQUIDATION_FEE_EBP`) of the notional it retires) for a seizure that nets against the vault's inventory; the call's inline unload adds whatever 2 bp of what it sells exceeds those rewards beyond the nettings' 2 bp; an account whose position is worth less than $5 counts for at most its own equity; capped at insurance free capital |
| `MAX_DEFERRED_LIQUIDATIONS` | 16 — opposite-side liquidations the vault may leave open at once because netting them would breach the fund's reserve; when full, a newcomer replaces the entry with the smallest loss beyond its collateral. Bounded by the auction rather than the gate: a batch may scan the whole cohort for each listed account |
| `MAX_LIQUIDATION_PRICE_AGE` | 10 min — oldest stale-fallback price `liquidate()` will judge solvency against; past it the caller must supply a `priceUpdate` |
| Vault-unload keeper reward | 2 bp of unloaded notional on any amount, raised to a $0.25 floor (`UNLOAD_BOUNTY_MIN`) when the call unloads ≥ $1,250 (`UNLOAD_BOUNTY_MIN_NOTIONAL`, where 2 bp overtakes the floor) or clears the inventory — so a thin-book keeper is never unpaid, and slicing one unload into many earns no premium. Paid from insurance, capped at its free capital |
| ADL trigger / release | opens when expected loss > free capital above the reserve, or while any deferred liquidation is under water; releases once that capital could finish the remaining inventory at the unload band's edge and no deferred liquidation is under water |
| ADL auction | 25× score → ~0, quadratic, 10 min; restarts at 25× after a ≥ 1 h price gap or a side flip; ≤ 25 winners per cohort per call (the inventory's opposite side against the inventory, its own side against the deferred liquidations) |
| ADL timeout | 24 h, then a 30 min fresh-priced runway → settlement window opens at the live price |
| ADL executor reward | 0.1% of averted bad debt across both cohorts, plus max($0.05, 2 bp at the mark of the notional the batch retires with it — the size its counterparties closed or the overlap netted against the inventory) for every deferred account the batch seizes, $0.05 alone for one it moves into the inventory; the $0.05 capped at the account's own equity below $5 of notional; the two are capped together at insurance free capital |
| Insurance target | `max(M × INSURANCE_TARGET_KAPPA_BP, realized-drift EMA)`, clamped to 2% … 10% of OI. The forecast leg (1× of `M`) is the same reading maintenance margin is set to, so coverage per unit of maintenance margin is constant across pairs and regimes; the drift leg is that same loss measured directly. Competing by `max` rather than summing, because the two estimate one quantity — what a liquidation loses beyond maintenance margin |
| Insurance LP | min $5 deposit, at most 100 deposits per address across the current and previous three UTC days (`MAX_DEPOSITS_PER_WINDOW`); 13-day cooldown + 3-day window (`INSURANCE_WITHDRAWAL_COOLDOWN` / `INSURANCE_WITHDRAWAL_WINDOW`); in the window a lock, then the paying call ≥ 5 min after it and after the last ≥ 2% price jump, and ≥ 30 min after the last reopen (`INSURANCE_WITHDRAWAL_CLAIM_DELAY` / `INSURANCE_WITHDRAWAL_REOPEN_CLAIM_DELAY`), paying the lower of the two values; rate limits min(0.5% OI, 5% fund) / 6 h (below target), max(1% OI, 10% fund) (above, where the OI is the lower of the period's opening reading and the open interest held for the whole trailing day, `Vault.trailingOiSize`, read at the paying call); while the vault holds inventory, a paying call pays at most free capital less the reserve and the cost of finishing the inventory, valued together at whichever edge of the unload band (min(`LIQ_MAX_SLIPPAGE_BP`, stored MMR) either side of the mark) makes their sum larger, and a larger claim is paid that far with the rest left requested |
| Insurance share pricing | mint and redeem against free capital (fund less the seizure earmarked against held inventory) plus the inventory's net result at the mark (its PnL from the estates' entry, plus the seizure and the ledger's arrears that back it) — a deposit counts gain and loss, a redemption counts loss only; with no inventory held a deposit still counts the ledger's arrears to the fund and a redemption does not. The loss deduction stops at the capital above the reserve (`ADL_RESERVE_BP`), since the auction charges the rest to winners. A redemption on a live pair always needs a fresh mark; a deposit needs one only while the vault holds inventory, and reads no price at all when it is flat |
| Maker insurance fee | 0.5 bp flat |
| Taker insurance fee | base 0.5 bp … 4 bp (scales with target ratio); below target × (1 + 49·deficit²), 50 bp hard cap; never below the base rate, and the part of a fill that closes the taker's position pays the base rate alone |
| `BUG_BOUNTY_TAX_BP` | 100 (1% of the insurance and integrator fee streams); the bug bounty address also receives the shares an insurer-vote levy forfeits |

## Oracle

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| `MAX_PRICE_STALENESS` | 3 s: the age bound on every submission path, and the settlement window before a UMA cutoff |
| `LIMBO_LEAD` | 6 s, two settlement windows: how long before any scheduled cutoff limbo begins, so the last block that trades is stamped a window and a second before the settlement window opens; a cutoff is scheduled at least `MIN_CUTOFF_LEAD` (7 s) after the scheduling block |
| `MAX_CONFIDENCE_BP` | 200 (2% confidence cap) |
| `REFRESH_BOUNTY` | $0.10 per `refreshPrice` call, paid from insurance |
| `REFRESH_BOUNTY_MIN_GAP` | 5 min — the pre-refresh stored price must be older than this while the vault holds inventory worth ≥ $1,250 (`UNLOAD_BOUNTY_MIN_NOTIONAL`) |
| `REFRESH_BOUNTY_HEARTBEAT` | 1 day — the gap that applies otherwise: a heartbeat that keeps a quiet pair's stored price well inside `ORACLE_DEAD_THRESHOLD`, so a long and a short parked in two wallets draw at most one bounty per day |
| `REFRESH_BOUNTY_MIN_INSURANCE` | $5,000 — the bounty pays only while the fund's free capital exceeds this; there is no open-interest floor, so a pair with positions but no trades still gets its heartbeat |

## Payouts

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Payout mechanism | every third-party payout (keeper rewards, integrator fees, bug-bounty tax, relayer fees, UMA proposer reward) accrues to a per-pair claimable balance — the lens's `claimableFees(pair, who)` to read, `claimFees()` to withdraw, `FeeAccrued` per accrual; open in every pair state |

## Listing & termination

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Listing | ≥ $4,000 = $1,000 UMA bond + ≥ $3,000 seed (`MIN_INSURANCE_SEED`); 48 h liveness |
| UMA identifier upgrade | $5,000 bond, 2-day liveness, then a 12-day activation timelock (`IDENTIFIER_UPGRADE_TIMELOCK`) after approval |
| `DVM_DISPUTE_GRACE` | 14 days on top of liveness before a **disputed** proposal may be discarded as unsettleable; shared by the deployment and identifier tracks (it bounds the DVM, not the claim) |
| Bond constants | floors, not fixed amounts — `requiredDeploymentBond()` / `requiredIdentifierUpgradeBond()` / `requiredTerminationBond()` each return `max(constant, UMA's live minimum)` |
| Proposer free text | listing `description` ≤ 200 bytes; termination `reason` ≤ 1,000 (`MAX_REASON_LENGTH`); restricted ASCII, no square brackets |
| Scheduled termination | $1,000 bond, 12 h liveness, `lastTradingTs` ≥ 2 h out (`INSURER_TERMINATION_MIN_LEAD`), enforceable as proposed by acceptance alone only when ≥ 12 h out; every claim opens an insurer vote on its cutoff; settles at the first Pyth print in the 3 s window ending at the cutoff (`MAX_PRICE_STALENESS`), with limbo from 6 s before it (`LIMBO_LEAD`); 12 h precise-tick grace from the cutoff, which moves to 7 s after acceptance when the proposed one is sooner; for a pair a vote halted under a claim UMA upheld, the grace counts from when the verdict became known (the end of the liveness, or the on-chain recording of a disputed result) if that is later |
| Stale-oracle termination | 21 days |
| Terminal settlement escape hatch | 14 days after `fixSettlementPrice`, `finalizeTermination` no longer waits for every position to be settled (deposits + all withdrawals stay frozen until finalize) |
| Insurer sweep grace | after an escape-hatch finalize, insurance withdrawals wait until every position is settled or 14 days beyond the hatch (`INSURER_SWEEP_GRACE`; 28 days after the fix at the latest) |
| Insurer vote | bare proposal: $1,000 bond, the UMA termination bond's floor (`TERMINATION_PROPOSAL_BOND`), cutoff 2 h to 14 days after the proposal (`INSURER_TERMINATION_MIN_LEAD` / `INSURER_TERMINATION_MAX_LEAD`); a UMA claim's vote carries no bond and opens 14 days before its cutoff, or at filing if that is later; 60% of the share snapshot; voting and the latch close 1 h before the cutoff (`INSURER_TERMINATION_LATCH_LEAD`), so every vote has at least an hour; 4-day share maturity at the window's opening (`INSURER_SHARE_MATURITY_PERIOD`; lots tracked 18 days); a voter may back at most 8 live proposals (`INSURER_MAX_LIVE_VOTES`); a vote-scheduled pair waits for its claim's verdict up to 14 days from the filing (`CLAIM_VERDICT_GRACE`); a costed outcome — a rejected claim, or an unjudged one (a bare vote, or a claim with no verdict by that grace) unless the vote met a cutoff UMA accepted within 14 days of its own — locks the yes-voters 13 days after the fix (`INSURER_POST_FIX_LOCK`, the withdrawal cooldown) and levies 50% of their voted shares on a rejected claim, 50% × the dissent fraction unjudged and never less than 10% (`INSURER_LEVY_BP` / `INSURER_LEVY_FLOOR_BP`), credited to the bug bounty address; settlement without an upheld claim at the median of 5 prints chained from the cutoff, each the first at least 10 minutes after the one before (`SETTLEMENT_SAMPLE_COUNT` / `SETTLEMENT_SAMPLE_SPACING`); stored-price last resort 21 days after the cutoff (`ORACLE_DEAD_THRESHOLD`); when the schedule met a cutoff UMA accepted, every sample is capped at that cutoff (`umaCutoffCap`) and the last resort opens 12 h after it if that is sooner (`SCHEDULED_TERMINATION_GRACE`) |
| Balance-check tolerance | 0.1% (`USDC_BALANCE_TOLERANCE_BP`) |
| UMA proposer reward | 0.1% of fund, cap $100, once per pair, and capped at the fund's free capital like every other payout — the seizure earmarked against held inventory settles that inventory, and a reward drawn from it would resurface as a shortfall on the next netting or auction close |
